# Destination photographs

The destination cards use locally hosted WebP photographs from Wikimedia Commons.
All four originals were published under CC0 by Bernard Gagnon (Bgag).
Images are resized to a maximum width of 2400 pixels and encoded at 92% quality.
The cards crop the photographs to fit their layout.
